Hesperiae Amantes - Lovers of the west

Vetus Cultura Renata - The old culture reborn 

Hesperianism - The ideology of the West lovers. From latin Hesperiae Amantes: This translates to "lovers of Hesperia," with Hesperia being a poetic name of the Western lands in Greco-Roman mythology.

Hesperia resurget!: This translates to "Hesperia will rise again!" conveying a sense of determination and confidence.



I was thinking about education and Western culture a lot lately, and I think they are inextricably connected with each other. They ware both great and now they suck. When they are in good shape the world flourish and when they are broken the world is in chaos and human suffering drastically increases. It very much looks to me that we are just before this awful period of wars, hunger and killing each other for no reason. I don't think it has too be like that. Civility is what makes us humans and helps us communicate and corporate with each other effectively. We simply figured out that it is much more reasonable and prosperous to negotiate and deal with each other then to fight each other. 

It wasn't always like this. I would even say that we are incredibly lucky to live in very unusual times when we don't have to fight and fear for our lives every day. But it was long and gradual process. First, instead of living alone we connected into tribes (probably somewhere around being a monkey stage), then ware living in those tribes and cooperate inside them but usually we ware killing and raping the other tribes (or they ware killing and raping us). 








I stole the structure from the [agile manifesto](https://agilemanifesto.org) and wrote something like this:

We are uncovering better ways of developing education and culture by doing it and helping others do it.

Through this work we have come to value:

- **Power** over vulnerability
- **Freedom** over equality
- **Truth** over correctness
- **Science** over religion
- **Producing** over consuming
- **Transparency** over trust
- **Reasoning** over feeling
- **Innovation** over tradition
- **Action** over contemplation
- **Cooperation** over competition
- **Rebellion** over obedience
- **Independence** over conformity
- **Order** over anarchy
- **West** over east
- **Humanity** over earth

_That is, while there is value in the items on the right, we value the items on the left more._

We also believe the following statements to be true:

- Education should be Declarative not Imperative
- Graduation = limitation
- Equal chances = unequal outcomes
- Survival of the fittest = Failure of the fattest
- Stagnation = Death
- Civilizations are extremely rare and fragile
- A System that Serves the Few Breeds Revolution for the Many
- Freedom of speech is non-negotiable
- All degrees are fake
- Science is the new God
