We live in times of abssance of good causes to fight for and it drives people crazy. So in this pathetic desperation we look for stupid causes and fiercely fight for them. Let's use feminism as an example. 

First Wave Feminism (late 19th - early 20th centuries)

Problems:
No property rights for women
No access to higher education
Systematic legal discrimination against women
Societal norms heavily favoring men

Second Wave Feminism (1960s - 1980s)
Problems:
No gender equality
Sexual freedom and reproductive rights
Workplace discrimination
Lack of bodily autonomy for women aka abortion rights
Widespread gender-based discrimination

Third Wave Feminism (1990s - 2010s)
Problems:
Addressing intersectionality
Expanding feminism beyond primarily white middle-class concerns
Challenging traditional gender roles
Marginalization of diverse voices within the feminist movement
Persisting traditional gender norms and expectations


Fourth Wave Feminism (2010s - present)
Problems:
Manspalining (when man have an opition and want to share it)
Manterrupting
Fatphobia
