# The Fitarian Manifesto


When I came after the summer to the UK I visited our campus shop called Costcutter and I was amazed by discovering that there is syriously nothing I can eat there if I want to stick to my diet and continue my weight loss plan. 
<details>
  <summary>Stats for gym nerds here </summary>
    Weight: 82kg 
    Bodyfat: 22% (Don't dirty bulk kids, it's stupid AF)
    Kcal: 2000
    Protein: 175g
    Carbs: 250g
    Fat: 33g
</details>

And at the same time on like half of the products there are a huuudge labels like "Protein", "Smart Choice", "Healthy", "Low Fat" and so on. But in reality when you look at the macro table it's pure shit. It's the same macro like a fucking snikers or mars, but it's even worse because when you eat snikers you are not promised to lose weight and nobody belives it's a smart choice. 
I was fucking angry as hell. It's like somebody spit in my face and think I'm stupid. Are those companies really think people are so stupid they won't notice that "Protein" bar has 5g of protein and 30g of fat or they are so stupid that they don't think it matters. 

Also when I'm looking for a quick snack or meal I don't want to look everytime at the macro table to figure out if they are trying to fuck me or not. I would like to have a quick label that shows me it's ok for my diet. But forcing big corporations to clearly show their macros will be very very hard. That's why I think that we as a fitness community that belives in science, phisics, macronutiens and calories instead of Paleo, Veganism or being fat because of genetics, should start our own diet style. Let's call it Fitarianism or Hotarianism (becaue it makes you hot, get it?), or Juicyarianism (because it will make your ass / chest / arms juuuuuicy as fuck).

How will this "new magic diet" work? Very simple. You can everything you want unless it fits your macro. As always. But we need to make and agree on some universal standards that meal in the restaurant should be meet. It sound complicated but let me explain it. 

There are standarized meal sizes from XS to XXL, and each meal size can get + or - or stay as it is deppending on it's PPM (protein per meal). Here is the table. 

| Size | Weight KG | Weight LBS | Calories | Meals per day | Kcal per meal | PPM - | PPM | PPM + |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| XS | 50 | 110 | 1500 | 4 | 375 | 20 | 30 | 40 |
| S | 60 | 132 | 2000 | 4 | 500 | 25 | 35 | 45 |
| M | 70 | 154 | 2500 | 4 | 625 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
| L | 80 | 176 | 3000 | 4 | 750 | 35 | 45 | 55 |
| XL | 90 | 198 | 3500 | 4 | 875 | 40 | 50 | 60 |
| XXL | 100 | 220 | 4000 | 4 | 1000 | 45 | 55 | 65 |



I'm active male 82kg so for maintanace I will probably go for 4 L size meals. If I want to cut 4 M or S meals and if I want to bulk 4 XL or XXL meals. 

Of course I still will be cooking some meals at home and count calories but when I'm in the hurry or I just want to eat out with my friends (like a normal human being) I will be able to do that without eating like 60g of fat in one meal. And I know it's not perfect and everyone has an indvidual calorie intake but this system can at least show food companies and restaurants what we want and in what diraction they should go. Because right now I just can't eat out and 100% of snaks/fast meals in the shops are not for us and we should do something about it. So what you can do to make it a reality? Send it to your favourite fitness influencer and tell him to make video about it. Post it on the instagram, tik-tok or whatever you use. When you are in the shop or a restaurant ask if they have a Fitarian option. Don't be like a blue hair vegan and don't cry when they don't have it, just say "ok, maybe next time" and just politely explain what is it and why it's important.
